Roll Rampage Support

Questions, bugs or ideas? Email support.rollrampage@gmail.com. Please include your iPhone model and iOS version.

I deleted a photo by mistake

Open the Photos app › Albums › Recently Deleted. Deleted photos stay there for 30 days and can be recovered.

Does deleting here also delete from iCloud?

Yes, if iCloud Photos is turned on. Roll Rampage works with your Photos library, so a deleted photo is removed from all devices signed in to your iCloud account, and the iCloud storage is freed too. It stays in "Recently Deleted" for 30 days first.

Why does iOS ask me to confirm deletion?

iOS always asks before an app deletes photos. Roll Rampage collects everything in its Trash Bin first, so you only confirm once per Empty Trash, however many photos are in it.

I can't trash a photo

Favorites are protected. Double-tap the photo to remove it from Favorites first. If the "INSERT COIN" screen appears, you've used the 100 free trashes.

I bought the full game but it's locked

Open Settings (Menu button › Settings) › Restore Purchases, signed in with the Apple Account you bought it with.

How do "Blurry & Dark" and "Similar Photos" work?

Tap the tile in the Menu to start a scan. It runs on your iPhone while the app is open, starts with your newest photos and continues next time you open the app. Large libraries take a while the first time; after that, only new photos are checked. Blurry & Dark needs iOS 18 or later.

How do I add the widget?

Open Roll Rampage once, then touch and hold your Home Screen, tap Edit › Add Widget and choose Roll Rampage. It also works on the Lock Screen.

Can I change the order of the months?

Yes: Menu › Settings › Play Order. Choose Back in Time (newest first), Oldest First or Random.

Can I play in German or Spanish?

Roll Rampage follows your iPhone's language. To change only this app, open the Settings app › Apps › Roll Rampage › Language.
