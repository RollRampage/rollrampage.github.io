Privacy Policy

Roll Rampage · Last updated: 9 October 2026

Short version: Roll Rampage does not collect any data. Your photos never leave your iPhone.

Your photos

With your permission, Roll Rampage reads your photo library on your device so it can show you your photos, and deletes the photos you choose to delete. Deletion always requires your confirmation in a system dialog, and deleted photos remain in "Recently Deleted" in the Photos app for 30 days. Photos are never uploaded, copied to a server or shared.

On-device photo analysis

If you start the "Blurry & Dark" or "Similar Photos" bonus stages, Roll Rampage analyses your photos on your iPhone with Apple's Vision framework to find blurry, dark or near-identical shots. The analysis runs entirely on your device. Its results are stored only on your iPhone and are never uploaded.

Game progress

Your progress (which photos you reviewed, your Trash Bin, scores, achievements and settings) is stored only on your iPhone. It is deleted when you delete the app. We have no access to it.

No accounts, analytics, ads or tracking

Roll Rampage has no user accounts, contains no analytics or advertising SDKs, does not track you across apps or websites, and does not connect to our own servers. We do not collect, store or sell personal data.

Widget and sharing

The optional Home Screen and Lock Screen widget shows a summary of your progress, such as photos left and your streak. The app passes this summary to the widget on your iPhone only. If you tap Share, the app creates an image with your game stats (it contains none of your photos) and hands it to the iOS share sheet; you decide where it goes.

Purchases

The optional "Full Game" purchase is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment details. Apple's privacy policy applies to the purchase: apple.com/legal/privacy.

Notifications

If you turn on the optional bin reminder, the app schedules a local notification on your device. No data is sent anywhere for this.

Children

Roll Rampage does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Because Roll Rampage does not collect personal information, we hold nothing about you that could be accessed, corrected or deleted. If you have a question about privacy, contact us using the details below. You can also contact the New Zealand Office of the Privacy Commissioner (privacy.org.nz) or, if you live in the EU, your local data protection authority.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.
